## Chosen approach: Garden Atelier Editorial

### Design Movement
**Contemporary craft editorialism**, drawing from independent floral studios, Japanese packaging restraint, and carefully composed print catalogues.

### Core Principles
1. Treat each crochet piece as a small crafted object, not a commodity.
2. Use intentional asymmetry and generous breathing room to create a calm, premium pace.
3. Combine material warmth with crisp editorial structure: tactile imagery, delicate rules, and precise typesetting.
4. Turn handcraft into an experience of discovery through gentle, purposeful interactions.

### Color Philosophy
The site uses warm oat, chalk, petal pink, and forest ink so the palette echoes natural fibre, fresh flowers, and a maker’s studio. The emotional contrast comes from an ownable **Croshii Moss** accent, a grounded dark green that gives calls to action and navigation a quietly luxurious authority rather than relying on harsh black.

### Layout Paradigm
The page is a **vertical studio walk** rather than a conventional centered sales grid: a compact editorial header leads into an asymmetric split hero; a staggered product “shelf” follows; process, gifting, and material stories are interleaved as wider visual pauses. Catalog information sits beside images as labels, mirroring a gallery display.

### Signature Elements
1. Fine stitched-line borders and curved yarn-path strokes that travel across selected sections.
2. Small maker’s labels with product codes and numbered editorial markers.
3. Botanical silhouette shapes and off-white paper panels layered over soft colour fields.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like handling a thoughtfully packaged piece: subtle lift, reveal, and underline gestures rather than flashy effects. Navigation is direct, and ordering actions lead clearly to the existing WhatsApp channel.

### Animation
At page entry, content may appear in short 50–80ms staggered fades with a 200–280ms custom ease-out. Product cards lift 3–5px with their image subtly scaling to 1.025 on hover. The yarn-path elements can drift very slowly only when motion is allowed; all nonessential movement is removed for `prefers-reduced-motion`.

### Typography System
**DM Serif Display** handles expressive product stories and editorial headings; **Manrope** carries body copy, labels, and controls for clarity. Headlines use restrained, high-contrast scale with occasional italic accents; uppercase labels use generous tracking and no more than a 12px visual size.

### Brand Essence
**Croshii makes handcrafted crochet gifts and everyday objects for people who value warmth with a point of view.**

Personality: **tactile, thoughtful, quietly joyful**.

### Brand Voice
Croshii speaks with simple sensory language and earned confidence—never generic, overly sales-led, or whimsical for its own sake.

> “A small thing, made slowly, meant to stay close.”

> “Bring your idea. We’ll work the warmth into every stitch.”

### Wordmark & Logo
The Croshii wordmark pairs a restrained, high-contrast serif with a circular yarn-ball symbol embedded in the ‘O’ space. The standalone logo is a looping, one-line yarn knot that reads as both a flower bud and a tiny crafted object, without using text.
